§ 01

About the work

I build the layer between raw data and real decisions — pipelines that hold up, models that get used, and analyses that hold their shape in front of a stakeholder.

Before Carnegie Mellon, I spent close to three years at Deloitte as a Data Analyst and AI & Analytics Consultant. I worked across pharma, retail, QSR, and healthcare — building ETL on Python and SQL, designing data models on Databricks and BigQuery, and shipping Tableau and Power BI products for cross-functional teams.

At CMU, I went deeper on the modeling and applied AI side — LLMs, transformers, RAG, AI agents, and AI governance — because that is where data work is going, and because I wanted the foundation to follow it there.

I am at my best when the question is ambiguous, the data is messy, and someone needs an answer they can defend.
